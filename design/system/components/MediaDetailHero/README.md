# MediaDetailHero

The collapsing stage at the top of every media detail page: backdrop, room light, scrim and poster row at rest, folding into a 52pt glass title bar as the body scrolls.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/media_detail_view.dart` (`mediaDetailHeaderSliver`, `_MediaDetailHeader`, `MediaDetailLoadingView`), with `media_detail_header_metrics.dart`, `media_detail_poster_row.dart`, `media_detail_hero_summary.dart`, `media_detail_back_button.dart`, `media_detail_figure_plate.dart` and `GlassSurface` from `glass_app_bar.dart`, by `test/design_catalog/media_detail_hero_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Extents**: expanded 236, collapsed 52 (`MediaDetailHeaderMetrics.baseExpandedHeight` / `baseCollapsedHeight`), both plus the top safe area and both grown with reading size: the hero on the 1.6× clamp (`heroMaxScaleFactor`), the bar on the 1.3× single-line chrome clamp. Collapse range 184 at the default size. Everything is driven by one progress `t` (`MediaDetailHeroPhase`) on staged intervals: scrim deepens over 0–0.80, bar reveals over 0.62–0.92 (ease-out cubic), poster scales 1 → 0.86 over 0.20–0.90, room light 0.22 → 0.12 over 0.30–0.75. The poster row is never faded, since it rides the rising bottom edge plus an exit push (0.7 of the range) and is clipped out of the header's box, the same Hero source it always was.
- **Art group**: the backdrop (`CachedNetworkImage`, `BoxFit.cover`, through the pinned image cache) stops one gutter (`AppSpacing.lg`, 16) short of the header's bottom, parallaxes at 0.35 of scroll, and carries the room light when an accent is given: `AppGradients.serviceGlow(accent, center: topRight, radius: 1.3)` (the lamp). `ServiceKey.accent` is the lamp for every service, until a title's light is derived from the artwork. With no backdrop the fallback stage is `surfaceContainerHighest` → `surface`, then at 22% either the poster (120 wide, when a `posterUrl` is given) or the page's glyph at 90 in `onSurfaceVariant`, then `surface` 4% → `surface` over it. A failed backdrop load paints `surfaceContainer`.
- **Route settle**: while the route pushes, the art group fades in and eases from 1.06 to 1.0 on `AppAnimation.emphasizedCurve`, behind the poster's Hero flight. Identity once the route has landed.
- **Scrim** (`AppGradients.heroScrim`): bottom-up `surface` → `surface` at 85% (`opacityHeroScrimRest`) at 0.35 → clear; at full depth the mid stop is opaque and the top holds 35% (`opacityHeroScrimDeep`). Below it a 16pt **seam** fades `surface` to nothing so the room's ambient glow reads through (same RGB at both stops, only alpha moves). Never a blur.
- **Poster row** (`MediaDetailPosterRow`), inset 16 left, right and bottom, bottom-aligned: the 82 × 123 lead (`MediaPosterCard`), or 92 × 92 with `MediaDetailPosterRow.circular` (`MediaPosterCard.circular`); `AppSpacing.md` (12); then the summary: status badge, `AppSpacing.xs` (4), title in `titleLarge` at w600 with 1.15 leading (two lines, then ellipsis), 4, metadata in `bodyMedium` `onSurfaceVariant` (`MediaMetadataLine`, one line), `AppSpacing.sm` (8), at most three chips wrapping at 4. The whole row lays out on the 1.6× hero clamp, the same one the band grew by.
- **Figure plate** (`MediaDetailFigurePlate`, posterless pages such as Bazarr), the same 82 × 123 slot: `surfaceContainerHigh`, `AppRadius.md`, `serviceGlow(accent, center: topLeft, radius: 1.2, alpha: 0.14)`; the figure in `headlineSmall` (26, w600) with tabular figures, coloured by `ServiceTheme.onTint` for that surface at 0.14: the primary ink, since the accent is the lamp; 4; the label in the micro role (`AppTheme.chrome`, 11 / 600 / 14, the role's own tracking), in sentence case (*Missing*), `onSurfaceVariant`. Silent to screen readers.
- **Back button** (`MediaDetailBackButton`): a 36 plate inside a 48 target, plate edge on the 16 gutter (left inset 10). Over art: `surface` at 0.62 (the computed AA floor over any artwork) carrying a 22pt `chevron_left_rounded` in `onSurface`, top inset 12 below the safe area. In the bar: centred ((52 − 48) / 2 = 2), plate released to nothing from reveal 0.82. The glyph never inverts, in either theme.
- **Collapsed bar**: `GlassSurface`, a gradient and not a blur: `surface` 55% → 28% at 0.55 → clear (light 60% / 32%). Title centred in `titleMedium` w600, one line, ellipsised, clearing 70 each side (10 + 48 + 12, `MediaDetailBackButton.barClearance`), laid out on the 1.3× clamp. While loading with no title yet, a 140-wide shimmer line stands in.
- **Loading**: `MediaDetailLoadingView` builds the same header sliver with a shimmer poster card, a shimmer badge and blank title and metadata lines, so the swap to data moves nothing.
- **Overscroll**: on bouncing physics (iOS, macOS) pulling down stretches the art; the poster row follows at 0.35 of the stretch.

The consumer provides, on `MediaDetailView`: `posterRow` (a `MediaDetailPosterRow` with badge, title, metadata items, chips and a poster or figure plate), `backdropUrl`, `posterUrl` (the fallback stage's faded poster), `title` (the bar's), `accent` and the required `heroFallbackIcon`. Titles, years and counts in the catalog are illustrative.

Semantics: exactly one title is spoken at a time: the hero title and the bar title are both headers and hand off on the same `barReveal ≥ 0.5` threshold. The poster is excluded (the title beside it names the region). Reduce Motion flattens parallax, stretch, poster scale, the route settle and the poster row's exit push; scrim, bar and glow still track the finger, because they are a remap of the finger's own position.

Do: pass the page's service accent (the lamp) and its own fallback glyph. Don't: wrap the poster row in an `Opacity` (it is a Hero source), or add a blur behind the bar.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room; the hero also on the desktop.

- Expanded, under a 47pt safe area, mid-collapse, collapsed; a long title; no backdrop with a poster, no backdrop and no poster; the circular artist row; `MediaDetailLoadingView`.
- Text at 200%: expanded with a long title, collapsed, and the loading view.
