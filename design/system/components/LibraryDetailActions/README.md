# LibraryDetailActions

The deck of a Radarr, Sonarr or Lidarr detail page: one status in, one promoted verb out, a sentence saying what follows, and everything else behind an overflow.

Hand-written from `lib/core/widgets/library_detail_actions.dart` and `header_action_row.dart`, with the promotion ladder in `lib/core/status/media_action.dart`, the sheets and dialogs it opens (`media_profile_selector.dart` → `showMediaValueSheet`, `media_search_popup_menu.dart`, `delete_media_dialog.dart` → `showAppConfirmDialog` in `app_dialog.dart`).

- **Band** — a row, then `space-sm` and the consequence in `body-small` `on-surface-variant` (three lines max). Every control is 48 high, `radius-md`, `space-sm` apart. Never padded by the caller: the spine owns the gutter.
- **Promoted verb** — `FilledButton.icon`, full remaining width, the service accent as fill with its label chosen by `foregroundOn` (ink on Radarr amber), padding `space-md` horizontal, 18px glyph, `label-large` 600, wraps to two centred lines rather than breaking a word. Busy: the glyph becomes a spinner and **nothing in the band takes a tap**. Landed: a success check (walked by `onTint` against the accent fill) holds for the confirmation interval.
- **Secondaries** — tonal: `on-surface` at 6%, 1px `outline-variant`, `on-surface-variant` ink. With a primary: one 48 × 48 icon disc (Interactive search ranks first). Without: two labelled buttons sharing the width, padding `space-sm`, no glyph (the words are the affordance). Delete is never visible.
- **Overflow** — a 48 disc with `more_horiz` opening the *Actions* sheet (subtitle: the title): Interactive search, Automatic search, Monitor / Stop monitoring, Manual import, then a divider and Delete last in `error`. Rows are 48, 18px glyph, `space-md`, `body-large` 600. The sheet spends itself on one tap.
- **Ladder** (real strings) — Missing: *Automatic search*, *Radarr is hunting a release for this title.* Upgradable: *Interactive search*, *The file is below your quality cutoff, so Radarr keeps looking.* Unmonitored: *Monitor*, *Radarr isn't searching for this title.* Import blocked: *Finish import*. In flight: *Open queue*. Available: no primary, *Radarr has everything it expects, so it isn't searching.*
- **Quality profile sheet** — one field per sheet: title *Quality profile*, subtitle *Currently HD-1080p*, 48-high option rows, 18px glyph, `space-md`, `body-large`. Selected: `check_circle` and a 600 label in the accent walked for `surface-container`; others `circle_outlined` in `on-surface-variant`, label 500.
- **Search menu** — 20px (18 in rows) search icon button opening a Material 3 menu: *Automatic search*, *Interactive search*; a 20px, 2px spinner while searching.
- **Delete dialog** — `AlertDialog`, `radius-lg`, `surface-container` (dark) / `surface` (light), 24 padding: `warning_amber` walked red (`statusToneColor` against the dialog's own surface), `space-md`, *Delete Nosferatu?* in `title-large` 600 (two lines max, `on-surface`); *This will remove this movie from Radarr.* in `body-medium`; `space-md`; two leading-checkbox rows (*Add list exclusion* / *Prevent this movie from being re-added by lists*; *Delete files* / *Permanently delete downloaded files from disk*). Checking the danger option turns its label `body-large` 600 and subtitle to the same walked red, and shows the banner: unwalked `error` fill at 10%, 35% border, `radius-sm`, padding `space-md`, 20px `warning` glyph and *This cannot be undone.* in `body-medium` 600, both walked against the banner's own 10% wash. Actions: *Cancel* (text, `primary`), *Delete* (filled, unwalked `error` background, label from `foregroundOn`).

The consumer provides `service`, the resolved `status`, `mediaTitle`, `isMonitored`, `isBusy` / `isConfirmed` and the callbacks; a missing callback withholds that action entirely rather than disabling it. The title and profile names are illustrative (the profiles are Radarr's defaults).

Semantics: disc glyphs carry the accessible name (*Interactive search*, *More actions*); the confirmed glyph announces *<label> complete*.

Do: map every in-flight flag onto `isBusy`; walk every destructive-action red against the surface it lands on. Don't: add a quality-profile control here — a detail edits in place, on its value row; don't paint a status red for anything other than an irreversible action or unreachability.
