# MediaPage

The one page a title has, whatever service it was opened from: who the title is, the one thing to do with it, and what every server of the household knows about it, each in its own row.

Rendered from `lib/features/media/presentation/media_page.dart` (`MediaPage`) `media_add_sheet.dart` (`MediaAddSheet`) `media_copy_panel_card.dart` (`MediaCopyPanelCard`), `media_decision_card.dart` (`MediaDecisionCard`), `media_facts_sections.dart` (`MediaWatchProviders`, `MediaReleaseDates`) and `media_series_sections.dart` (`MediaNextAiringCard`, `MediaEpisodes`), with a series' seasons, next airing and next episode from `lib/features/media/domain/media_series.dart`, with the rows' words from `lib/features/media/domain/media_row_view.dart`, the lead from `lib/features/media/domain/media_lead.dart`, the identity, scores and marks from `lib/features/media/presentation/media_identity.dart`, the closed sections from `media_manage_summaries.dart` and the light from `lib/core/title_light.dart`, by `test/design_catalog/media_page_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Room** — `CupolaRoom.ground` under `AppGradients.ambientBackground` washed with the title's light (the lamp until the light is read, and for artwork with too little chroma). Gutter `AppSpacing.lg` (16); the back plate sits under the top safe area at `AppSpacing.md` (12).
- **Hero** — the backdrop in a slab 214 tall (`AppRadius.slab`, `AppElevation.hero` glowing with the light), the 88 × 132 poster (`AppRadius.poster`, the plate's outline in both rooms, `AppElevation.poster`) rising 52 out of its foot, and the title in the media role beside it, starting under the artwork, two lines then an ellipsis. The title sizes the hero: a second line pushes the meta line down. Under it, the meta line (year · runtime · genre, label role at 400, `inkSecondary`) and the format marks: `4K` when a copy is 2160p (a Radarr UHD quality or a media server's frame) and the range a media server reports (`HDR10`, `HDR10+`, `Dolby Vision`, `HLG`), each in a 5-radius outline at micro.
- **Missing artwork** — no backdrop: the poster stands in, blurred (σ 24) at 0.72 over the plate's well, decoded once at the poster's box. No artwork at all: the plate (`AppColors.plateWell` for the slab, `plateSurface` for the poster) with the kind's glyph in `plateDimInk`, dark in both rooms. While nobody has said who the title is, both shimmer, unless the tile the page was opened from lent its poster: that stands in until the title's own arrives. Who the title is comes from TMDB through Seerr, else from an arr's record, else from the media server's own item: its name, year, length and synopsis, and its poster, fetched with the server's credential (no backdrop: the poster stands in).
- **Scores** — one slab (`AppRadius.door`, `fillQuiet`, hairline) of four: TMDB, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, each figure in the figure role over its source in micro. A source with no figure reads `—`, never 0. The requester has no Metacritic. All four come from the record of an arr that holds the title; without one, TMDB's comes through Seerr. With neither Seerr nor an arr set up, the slab is not drawn: no source there has a score to give.
- **Lead** — one 52 button: `Play on X` / `Resume on X`, `Add to Radarr`, `Request`, or the action a copy asks for (`resolveMediaAction`); with nothing to play and a trailer, `Trailer` leads. In the dark room its fill is the light's action fill under the action ink; in the day room the light's tinted primary under white; unlit, the ink. A shimmer while a source that could change it has not answered. Under it, outlined: Trailer (when it does not lead), Watchlist (with Seerr: the watchlist of the user its connection acts as; the glyph fills while the title is on it, and it is heard as `On your watchlist`), Manage (operator only).
- **Request** — when Request leads (the requester, or an operator with Seerr and no arr), its words come from Seerr's state for the title, read as that Seerr numbers it (`seerrRequestWords`, shared with the Seerr page): `Request` with a plus opens the request sheet (`RequestBottomSheet` under `Request Nosferatu` and the meta line); a request that stands reads `Requested`, an answer the user cannot act on reads `Didn't go through` or `Can't be requested`, all legible on the room's strong fill. An account that may not ask for the kind keeps Request dimmed and tappable, with the server's reason. A title Seerr says is on the server, with Seerr's link to it (`mediaUrl`), leads with `Watch on Jellyfin` (Plex, Emby: the player Seerr is set up with) and an open glyph, and opens there. Under the buttons one line says where the title stands, a 7 dot in its tone (`Waiting for approval.`, `On its way.` in blue; the failed request in amber) or, for the requester, the backlog's ring and `Not on the server yet.`; while that line speaks, the requester reads no rows under it.
- **A copy that needs something** — when a Radarr copy leads, a panel takes the lead's place and the buttons under it (`MediaCopyPanelCard`, words from `mediaCopyPanel`): a slab (`AppRadius.door`, `fillQuiet`, hairline), the headline in the section role, who has it (`Radarr · qBittorrent`), what is happening in blue after a 7 dot (`Radarr asked your indexers at 20:53`), the sentence, the release (`MachineName.release`), the bar while it downloads, then what can be done. A fault (`Stuck at 47%`, `Failed`, `Needs a manual import`) is amber with its glyph, and its remedy is the one amber fill (`Try another download`, with what it does under it); a download client that does not answer is red and offers nothing. Calm states (`In Radarr · searching` with a spinner, `missing`, `not monitored`, `cutoff unmet`, `not out yet`, `Downloading · 62%`) offer quiet buttons: `Automatic search`, `Choose a release`, `Monitor`. A search is read from Radarr's commands and asked again while it runs; when it ends, the copy is read again.
- **A request that waits** — for the operator, each request Seerr holds for the title that waits for approval is a card (`MediaDecisionCard`, requests from `mediaPendingRequests`): a slab (`AppRadius.door`, `fillQuiet`, hairline), who asked in the section role (`Anna asked for it`, `in 4K` when it is; `Requested` where the connection does not see who), `Waiting for approval · 2 h ago` in blue after a 7 dot, then `Approve` with a check and `Decline` under it, the order and weight of the approval sheet. While the title is on no server (the lead would be Add, Request or Trailer) the card takes the lead's place and Approve is the page's one lit fill; the buttons under the lead stay. Under a lead that plays or fixes a copy, the card sits below it and Approve is quiet. Both answers go through the app's one Seerr write path (`ActivityActions`), whose question says where the request goes; then Seerr and Radarr are read again. An account that may not approve keeps both dimmed with the server's reason; the requester never sees them, and the line under the buttons does not repeat `Waiting for approval.`
- **On your server** — a heading with the count of services, then one group (`AppRadius.group`, `fillQuiet`, hairline) of rows split by hairlines. Each row: the connection's name in a 92 column, what it holds, and on the right the state's word after a 7 dot in its tone (a ring for the backlog). Four shapes (`MediaRowShape`): a **fact** (`2160p · HDR10 · 1 h 3 min left` · Available); a **failure** in its tone with the last answer and its time under it (`Did not answer since 18:12` / `Last known: 1080p · Available`) and, on the right, the remedy its cause names or `Retry`; **not set up**, dimmed (name 0.55, words 0.72), what the service would bring under it, and the `+ Set up X` pill (36 tall, `AppRadius.chip`, `fillControl`, edge); **through Seerr**, Seerr's word attributed (`through Seerr`) with `+ Add X to Cupola` under it. A row with a way forward is tapped whole: Set up and Add open the service's setup form, a remedy the connection's form, Retry asks that one connection again.
- **Manage** — operator only, and only when a copy is there to manage, closed: Files & quality, History, Subtitles (with Bazarr), Search releases, Remove, each with what it holds in one line. Files, History and Subtitles open in place and read on opening; under each copy's file, Files & quality edits what the arr does about it where it is read: `Quality profile` (a picker when there is more than one) and `Monitor` (a switch, in the add sheet's words), each shown at once and written to the arr, put back when the write fails, then `Import a file` (`From a folder on the server`), which opens the import wizard bound to that copy's connection; a copy with nothing on disk reads `Nothing on disk yet`; Search releases and Remove act on a single copy, or open on the copies when there are more (`Remove a copy`).
- **Add** — `Add to Radarr` leads a film no Radarr holds, with a plus. Its sheet (`MediaAddSheet` in `AppBottomSheet`, the film and its year under the title): the Radarr as pills when there are two, then one group — Quality profile and Root folder (the path in the mono role, its free space under it), each a picker when there is more than one; Monitor and Search when added, each a switch with what it means under it (`Grabs it and upgrades it to HD-1080p`, `Asks your 14 indexers right away`; unmonitored, Radarr never looks: `Needs Monitor on`). The button is the lead's, lit by the title: `Add and search`, or `Add to Radarr` when it will not search. Added, the page says `Added to Radarr` and reads every Radarr again.
- **Remove** — `Remove from Radarr` (`Stops Radarr looking for it`) asks through `showAppConfirmDialog`: `Remove Big Buck Bunny from Radarr?`, what follows (`Nothing of it is on your server yet.`, or which copy stays: `Radarr 4K keeps its copy.`), `Also delete 1.1 GB of files` when there are any, `Don't add it again`, and a confirm that names the whole of it (`Remove and delete 1.1 GB`); Cancel reads `Keep it`.
- **Where to watch** — after Manage (for the requester, after the servers' rows), with Seerr: a heading with the region Settings names (`Italy`), then one group with `Stream` and `Buy or rent`, each service by its TMDB logo (20, radius 5; a glyph when TMDB has none) and its name in the meta role. Facts, not controls: a row is read whole (`Stream: Netflix, Prime Video`) and never tapped. A region that names nowhere draws no section.
- **Reference** — the overview in the body role, the credits line, `Release dates` (a heading with the region, then `In cinemas`, `Digital`, `Physical` in one group, each date in the page's one date voice, `Wed 26 Sep 2012`: TMDB's for the region through Seerr, else Radarr's own dates with no region; a date nobody has draws no row), the cast rail, the collection and More like this (with Seerr).
- **A series** — the meta line says `Series · 2015– · 3 seasons` and two genres (`2015–2019` once it ended); the scores are two, TMDB's and Rotten Tomatoes' (through Seerr, `/tv/:id/ratings`). Play continues an episode: `Resume S2 E3` with `The Second Cycle · 5 min left` under it, or `Play S2 E4` with `Episode 4 · on Jellyfin` (`mediaNextEpisode`: the episode part-way through, else the first unwatched after the last watched, else the first). Under the buttons, the next episode to air in one slab (`MediaNextAiringCard`): the day in the section role (`Today`, `Tomorrow`, `Fri`, then `9 Oct`), `S3 E1 · premiere at 21:00`, what follows (`Sonarr looks for it once it airs` for the operator; from TMDB alone, a day without the hour and `Season 3 · 8 episodes announced`), and `Not aired` with the ring. In On your server, a media server counts its episodes by season against the episodes Sonarr says aired (`S1 10 of 10 · S2 3 of 10`; past three seasons, or without Sonarr, the total), and a Sonarr says one row per fact (`mediaRowViews`): `S2 E4–E5` Downloading, `S2 E6–E10 aired` with `5 missing` and the ring, and a stuck import as a **fault** row: `S1 · 10 upgrades need import` in amber after its dot, the sentence under it, and `Review the imports` as the one amber fill (the manual import review of that series on that Sonarr). With nothing of that, the row says its files. Then **Episodes**: the seasons as a segmented track (`fill.quiet` in a door, the chosen segment the ink; they share the width, and scroll past three), opening on the season Play continues, else the latest aired; one row per episode, `E3` in the meta role, the title, and one word after its dot or ring: `Watched`, `5 min left` with a bar in the ink, `Downloading 88%` with a bar in the info tone, `Finishing on the server`, `On your server`, `Missing`, `Not aired`. A stuck upgrade leaves the episode's word as it was. The requester reads `On the server`, `Not on the server yet` and, for a fault, `On its way`. Under the rows, `Search the 5 missing` (operator, with a Sonarr) asks that Sonarr for the season's missing episodes. A series' Manage is not drawn yet.
- **Collection** — one slab (`AppRadius.slab`, the plate's outline) over the collection's own artwork, veiled at 0.56 over the plate's well and decoded small, dark in both rooms: its name in the feature role and `5 of 8 on your server` in the label role at 600, both in `plateInk`, a chevron on the right. The figure counts the members Seerr holds, whole or in part; until Seerr has counted them, and when it could not, the band reads `Collection` and states no figure. The whole slab opens the collection.
- **Wider** — the page reads its own width, not the window's: in a tablet's detail pane it is the phone's column, wider. From 840 (`AppBreakpoints.touchRail`) it takes two tracks under the hero: scores, lead and the reference on the left, On your server and Manage in a 380 side track beside them (`AppSpacing.xl` between), More like this across both; the content stops at `AppBreakpoints.contentWell` (1140), centred. The slab grows with the column (its width / 2.6, from 214 to 420), and the poster and its rise with the slab, up to 1.5×.
- **Twice the reading size** — past 1.4× (the threshold `SectionHeader` and `MediaChildList` use) what sits side by side stacks: the title takes the whole width under the poster, the scores go two to a line, the buttons under the lead one to a line, and each row reads name, what it holds, then its state or its way forward.

The consumer provides only the title's `MediaKey` and, optionally, a `focus` (the connection the page was opened from), a `heroTag` and a `posterUrl` (the tile it was opened from). The page hangs at the title's own location (`/media/movie/653`) under the tab it was opened from (`ServiceRoutes.media`): the poster flies from the tile, and the tab keeps the page when the user leaves it and comes back. A movie a media server lists (its libraries, On deck, search) opens here when the server knows its TMDB id (`MediaKey.ofStreamItem`, `openStreamItem`), with that server's copy in focus; no poster flies from those tiles, since the server's artwork needs its credential. A series, an episode or a movie with no TMDB id keeps the server's own page. Everything else is read: `mediaRowsProvider`, the audience, the settings. Titles, figures and times in the catalog are illustrative; the light in the catalog is computed by `titleLightFromPixels` from the catalog's own artwork before the sheet is drawn.

Semantics: the title is the page's header; each row speaks one sentence (`Plex. Did not answer since 18:12. Last known: 1080p · Available. Retry`) and is one button when it leads somewhere, as is the collection (`Silent Horror, 5 of 8 on your server`); the scores speak `Rotten Tomatoes: 97`, `Metacritic: no score`. The artwork and the plate are silent.

Do: let the row decide its own shape and words (`mediaRowView`). Don't: paint a service's accent, draw a not-set-up service as answering, or build a control whose promise the page cannot keep: without Seerr, Trailer and Watchlist step aside.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room.

- Operator, the full household: Resume on Jellyfin lit by the backdrop, a fact with its picture, a failure with its last answer, two Radarr copies, Bazarr, Seerr not set up.
- Requester: the media servers only, no Manage, no Metacritic; and Request, over a title not on the server yet.
- No backdrop (the poster, veiled), no artwork (the plate), waiting.
- Media server only: no Seerr, no arr; the server names the title, with its synopsis and its poster.
- Series: Resume the episode, the next to air, Sonarr's facts one row each (a stuck import with its remedy), the episodes a season at a time.
- With Seerr: TMDB names the title, where to watch it, its release dates, and the collection band.
- Decision: Anna asked for a film nobody holds; the answer takes the lead's place.
- Tablet: a 592 detail pane, and the full 1180 screen in two tracks.
- Add: the sheet over a film nobody holds.
- Stuck: the fault leads with its remedy. Searching: Radarr asked its indexers.
- Text at 200%: the full household.
