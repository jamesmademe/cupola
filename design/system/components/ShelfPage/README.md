# ShelfPage

The page of a person and their shelf: an artist on Lidarr and their albums, an author on Readarr and their books, a person on TMDB and their work. A collection's page is the same room headed by its still (`ShelfPage.banner`). Who they are, what the shelf holds and where each work stands, then what the service does for them and the way to it. The Media page's room, without its slab: a person has no backdrop, their works do.

Lidarr's Library opens an artist's page (`/dashboard/lidarr/library/artist/:id`), Readarr's an author's (`/dashboard/readarr/library/author/:id`), each with the connection in `?i=`; every read and every write goes to that connection. A cast member, a search's person or a title's collection opens a person's or a collection's page beside the title, under the tab it was opened from (`…/media/person/:id`, `…/media/collection/:id`), read from the lens's Seerr. On a tablet on its side one opened from a list goes in the pane beside the list, as a title does (`MediaSplit.pageOf`), and one opened from a title in the pane stays there, Back returning to the title; a phone and a desktop window keep the whole page.

Rendered from `lib/features/media/presentation/shelf_page.dart` (`ShelfPage`, `ShelfIdentity`, `ShelfBanner`, `ShelfCover`, `ShelfWorkRow`, `ShelfSliverGroup`, `ShelfFolderRow`, `ShelfOpenIn`), `lib/features/music/presentation/artist_page.dart` (`ArtistPage`, `AlbumTracks`) with the albums' places and words from `lib/features/music/domain/artist_shelf.dart`, `lib/features/readarr/presentation/author_page.dart` (`AuthorPage`) with the books' from `lib/features/readarr/domain/author_shelf.dart`, `lib/features/discover/presentation/person_page.dart` (`PersonPage`) with the trades from `lib/features/discover/domain/person_shelf.dart`, and `lib/features/discover/presentation/collection_page.dart` (`CollectionPage`, its tally `CollectionTally`) with the parts from `lib/features/discover/domain/collection_shelf.dart`, by `test/design_catalog/shelf_page_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Room**: `CupolaRoom.ground` under `AppGradients.ambientBackground`, washed with the light of the first cover the page has drawn (else the person's photo; the lamp until one is read). One column, gutter `AppSpacing.lg`, never wider than `breakpoint.reading-well` (720) and centred in a wider window. Whatever scrolls under the clock fades into the room (`StatusBarScrim`). In a tablet's pane (`MediaPane`) the light comes up from the list's side over its first 110 points, as a title's does, and the back plate closes the pane or goes back in it.
- **Plates**: the back plate (`MediaBackPlate`) on the left and the page's other actions (`MediaPlate`, `more_horiz`) on the right, each 44, `fillControl`, `AppRadius.control`, the edge.
- **Identity**: a 92 disc: the person's photo, or their initials in the feature role on the light at 18% with its edge at 40% (`fillControl` and the edge, unlit). Beside it the name in the screen role, three lines then an ellipsis, and what the shelf holds in the label role at 400, `inkSecondary`: `3 albums on disk · 1 wanted · 1 announced`, `7 books · 6 on disk · 1 wanted`; a figure keeps its word on its line.
- **A person on TMDB**: under the name what they do (`Director`, from TMDB's department) and, beside the summary `1 of 3 on your server`, one 7pt dot per title of the trade they are known for, filled green for each on the server, a ring in `inkTertiary` for each that is not (`ShelfTally`; up to eight titles, past which the words stand alone). Then their trades, the one they are known for first and then whichever of `Directed`, `Wrote` and `Acted` they also did, each a heading with its count over a group of `SearchRow`s (`CatalogTitleRow`): the poster, `2012 · Movie · TMDB 6.6`, Seerr's word for the title, and the control at the end that its card describes. Newest first; a trade longer than ten shows its newest under `Show all 84`. An appearance as themselves (`Self`, a talk show, the news, reality television) is not their work. Then `About`: born, the place, the biography clamped to five lines; then `Credits from TMDB.` in the caption role.
- **A collection**: its still at 16:9, `AppRadius.door`, glowing with the light it gives the room; the name, `Collection · 8 movies · 2006–2022`. Its tally ([CollectionTally](../CollectionTally/README.md)): `5 of 8 on your server`, the strip of its movies and where each stands, and `5 on your server · 2 on the way · 1 waiting for approval` behind their dots. A request for one of its movies that waits for the operator follows as on a title's page (`MediaDecisionCard`, Approve the page's lit fill). Then `In release order`, the oldest first, in rows like the person's.
- **Covers**: on the artist's page, up to three album covers under the name, on disk first and newest first: a third of the column each (at most 160), `AppRadius.poster`, glowing with the light. Without artwork the strip is not drawn.
- **Actions** (the artist's): `Search albums` (Lidarr's search for every monitored album) and `Manage` (to Details), outlined, side by side. The more plate opens `Search releases`, `Import files` and `Remove from Lidarr`; the author's, `Search for every wanted book` and `Remove from Readarr`.
- **The albums**: in four places, each a heading and a group (`ShelfSliverGroup`: `AppRadius.group`, `door`, hairlines, rows built as they scroll in): **Coming** (announced, the soonest first: `Out Fri 2 Oct · not out yet` and `Lidarr looks for it once it's out`), **Wanted** (out, monitored, nothing on disk), **On disk** (a track or more), and **Unmonitored** behind `Show them`. A row (`ShelfWorkRow`): the 64 cover (`AppRadius.chip`; without one the plate, `plateWell` and a disc in `plateDimInk`, dark in both rooms), the title in the row role, `2010 · 5 tracks · 21 min` in the meta role, and the state after its dot or ring (`MediaStateWord`): `Complete` green, `4 of 5 tracks`, `Wanted`, `Unmonitored` with the ring, the queue's word in its tone (`Downloading 60%`). A word that needs the queue waits for it and reads `Unknown` once it failed.
- **An album open**: the row opens in place on its tracks (`AlbumTracks`), read on opening: the number, the title, the length, 44 tall with hairlines; a track not on disk is quieter when some are. Under them `5 of 5 tracks on disk`, then for an album with a gap `Search for this album` (the primary) and, for any album out, `Search releases`.
- **The books**: `Readarr is no longer maintained; finding new books may not work. Your books, and searching for a wanted one, still work.` once, under the name: a door (`AppRadius.button`, `fillQuiet`, hairline), the info glyph and the first clause in blue. Then **Books** `by year`, three to a row: each cover at 2:3 (`AppRadius.chip`), or a plate with the title and, under a hairline, the author (`plateSurface`, `plateInk`, dark in both rooms: artwork is not palette); a book not on disk has a dashed edge. Under it its state and its year (`Wanted 1863`, `1864`). Then **Wanted**: a card each, the small cover, the title, `Wanted · no file yet`, `1863 · quality profile eBook`, and `Search for this book`.
- **Details** (one group): `Monitored` (`Lidarr keeps these albums up to date`, `Readarr looks for missing books by this author`), the artist's `Monitor new albums` (`New releases are added and searched for on their own`; on writes `all`, off `none`), `Quality profile` (a picker when there is more than one, asked before it is written), and the folder in the mono role (`ShelfFolderRow`). Each switch shows its new state once the service has taken it.
- **Open in Lidarr**: the label role in `inkSecondary` with the open glyph, centred: the same page in the service's own web app.
- **Remove** (through `showAppConfirmDialog`): `Remove Jan Morgenstern from Lidarr?`, `Lidarr stops looking for their albums.` (`None of their albums are on your server yet.` when nothing is on disk), `Also delete 1.1 GB of files` when there are any, `Don't add them again`; Cancel reads `Keep them`.
- **Twice the reading size**: the name goes under the disc in the room role, the two actions one to a line, a setting's value under its label, and the books two to a row. A cover's lettering keeps the cover's size (it is a picture of a cover); the book's title is written under it instead, at the reader's size.

The consumer provides the record's id and, optionally, the connection (`instanceId`), the library's record to stand in while the page reads its own, and a `heroTag` (the disc takes the flight). Everything else is read: the record, the albums or books, the queue of that connection, its quality profiles.

Semantics: the name is the page's header; a work's row speaks one sentence and is one button when it opens (`Sintel, 2010, 5 tracks, 21 minutes, Complete`), a track speaks `Track 3, The Dragon, 5:36, on disk`, a book `Five Weeks in a Balloon, 1863, Wanted`. The disc, the covers and the plates are silent; the more plate says whose actions it holds.

Do: say where every work stands in the lexicon's words, and let a word that needs the queue wait for it. Don't: paint the service's accent, call a person "it", or offer a search for something the service cannot look up (Readarr finds no new book).

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room.

- The artist lit by the first cover: announced, on disk, the settings, the way to Lidarr.
- No artwork: the initials, the plates, the lamp; one album wanted.
- An album open on its tracks.
- The author: Readarr retired, the books by year, the wanted one dashed and searched for from its card.
- Text at 200%: the artist and the author.
- Reading the artist; a wide window, the column centred.
- A person by Seerr's credits: the role, the dots, the plus to Sonarr, Request, About and the credit line; the plus dimmed where nothing set up can add a movie; at twice the reading size.
- A collection (`ShelfPage.banner`): the still, the slab with the strip, the movies in release order.
- A person in a tablet's pane: Close, the light rising from the list's side.
