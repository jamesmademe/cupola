# MediaBrowseScaffold

The library wall shared by Movies, Series and Music: glass app bar, search field, KPI peek, filter chips with a pinned sort control, and a lettered poster wall that becomes a result grid while searching.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/media_browse_scaffold.dart` (with `lib/core/widgets/search_bar_header.dart`; results are `MediaGrid`, the peek is `ServiceKpiPeek`) by `test/design_catalog/media_browse_scaffold_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Page** — an `AmbientScaffold` in the service accent, body in the `AppBreakpoints.contentWell` (1140) well. The header (search, peek, chips) sits in a `NestedScrollView`'s header slivers, so it scrolls away with the wall rather than staying fixed.
- **App bar** — `GlassAppBar` (the `GlassSurface` gradient, no blur), `kToolbarHeight` (56), title in `title-large` 700 `on-surface`, one action: `Icons.history` (tooltip *Activity*) pushes `activityRoute`. While searching a leading `Icons.arrow_back` (tooltip *Exit search*) clears the query. `showAppBar: false` drops it for hosts that bring their own chrome, and `topPadding` reserves that much space, plus the top safe area, above the header.
- **Search** — `SearchBarHeader`: padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides and bottom. A Material `SearchBar`, min height 52, `radius-md`, `surface-container` fill with a 1px `outline-variant` edge, padding `space-sm`; leading `Icons.search_rounded` inset `space-sm`, in the accent walked by `ServiceTheme.onTint` against `surface-container`. With text, a trailing `Icons.close_rounded` clear button in `on-surface-variant`. Input is debounced 400ms. In the desktop shell the field registers as the screen's ⌘F target.
- **KPI peek** (hidden while searching) — whatever `kpiPeek` the screen passes, in practice a `ServiceKpiPeek` of the service's figures.
- **Filter strip** (hidden while searching) — padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, `space-md` bottom. Chips scroll, `space-sm` apart: padding `space-sm` × `space-md`, `radius-pill`, `label-medium` 600. Selected: solid accent, label by `ServiceTheme.foregroundOn`. Unselected: accent at 12% with the label walked by `onTint` against that wash on the page surface. Chips: *All*, *Available*, *Missing*, *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* (only when `cutoffUnmetIds` is non-null — an empty set still draws it), *Unmonitored*. *Available* and *Missing* partition the library through `mediaBrowseBucketOf`; *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* and *Unmonitored* are orthogonal to that partition.
- **Sort control** — pinned right of the scrolling chips and built only when there are two or more orders: *Title A–Z* (always first, the default) plus the screen's `sorts` (`MediaBrowseSort.dateAdded`, `.year`, `.size`, or a custom one). A `PopupMenuButton` (tooltip *Change sort order*) whose child is at least 44 × 44, padding `space-sm`: `Icons.swap_vert_rounded` 18, `space-xs`, then the order's label in `AppTheme.chrome` 600, both `on-surface-variant`.
- **Wall** — list padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, plus the nav bar's clearance at the foot on a phone. Under a `lettered` order (only Title A–Z) each first letter of the sort title, or `#` for anything else, heads a section in `title-large` 600 with `space-md` below it; sections are `space-xl` apart. Any other order is one unheaded run. Columns come from `MediaGrid.columnsFor` over the row's width (never fewer than 3), 12 between columns, 16 between rows, and the tiles take the width that is left: about 111 on a 390 phone, about 148 in the 1140 well.
- **Poster tile** — a 2 : 3 `ContentCard` under its own `Hero`, with an 8px `StatusBadge(iconOnly: true)` dot at 6 / 6 from the lower-left corner; `space-xs`; the title in `label-small` 600 `on-surface` and the subtitle (the year) in `label-small` `on-surface-variant`, one line each, ellipsised.
- **`tileShape: MediaBrowseTileShape.circle`** — Lidarr's wall: a square `MediaPosterCard.circular` with `Icons.person_outline` as its fallback, captions centred, and no dot — a circle has no corner to hold one, so the caption carries completeness in words.
- **Searching** — any non-empty query swaps the wall for a `MediaGrid` of `searchResultsProvider`: compact badges top-right, no captions. No results reads *No results found*; loading is a centred spinner.
- **Library states** — the wall goes through `AsyncValueWidget`: a centred spinner while loading, `AppErrorState` with *Retry* on a failure, `NotConfiguredPlaceholder` when the provider throws `<Service> not configured`. An empty library, or a chip that matches nothing, reads *No items found*.

The consumer provides `title` (*Movies*), `searchHint` (*Search movies...*), `serviceName`, `accentColor`, `activityRoute`, `navigationSection`, the hero tag prefixes for the wall and for search, the library, query and search-result providers, the settings selector for image auth, extractors for title, subtitle, sort title, images, id and status, `onItemTap`, the orders it can answer, the cutoff id set and the KPI peek.

Semantics: a tile is one button named by its full title, with *subtitle, status* as its value (*1922, Available*); the truncated caption and the dot are excluded. Chips are buttons carrying a selected state. The sort control's hint rides on its child (*Sorted by Title A–Z*), because a hint wrapped around a button is dropped. The search field is labelled *Search*.

Do: declare only the orders your model can answer, and pass `cutoffUnmetIds` as null until the service has answered. Don't: pin a tile width — the row resolves the count first and the tiles take what is left.
