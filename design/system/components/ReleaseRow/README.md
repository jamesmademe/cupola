# ReleaseRow

One release in the Interactive Search sheet: quality, protocol and score, the release name, size · age · indexer · peers, and a round Grab, colour-railed by what the *Arr would do with it.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/release_list_widgets.dart` (`ReleaseListItem`, `ScoreBadge`, `CustomFormatChip`, `InfoChip`) and `lib/core/widgets/interactive_search_sheet.dart` (`InteractiveSearchSheet`, its toolbar and its empty state) by `test/design_catalog/release_row_catalog_test.dart`. Release names, sizes, indexers and reasons in the catalog are illustrative; the indexer names are placeholders and the release group is `-GROUP`.

- **Card** — outer padding `AppSpacing.lg` (16) × `AppSpacing.xs` (4). `surfaceContainerHigh`, `AppRadius.md`, `AppElevation.level1`, a 1px border in `outlineVariant` at 50% (rejected: the tone at 28%), clipped. A 3pt rail down the left edge in the tone at 90%. Inner padding 12 left, 12 top, 8 right, 12 bottom. The whole card is an `InkWell` that toggles the details.
- **Tone** (`ReleaseStatusTone`, via `statusToneColor`): approved `success`, not approved `info`, rejected `warning` — never red. `releaseStatusTone(release)` is the one source for the rail, the border and the Grab.
- **Badge row** — wrapping, 4 apart: the quality pill (`primary` at 16%), the protocol pill (`onSurfaceVariant` at 10%, a 12pt `swap_vert_rounded` for Torrent or `cloud_download_rounded` for Usenet), and for a rejected release a `block_rounded` *Rejected* pill in the tone's fill at 10%. Every pill's label and glyph is its colour walked by `ServiceTheme.onTint` against the card's `surfaceContainerHigh` at that tint — the fill keeps the raw colour, the ink is measured. Pills: padding 8 × 2, `AppRadius.sm`, `labelSmall` w600. At the right, 4 away, the `ScoreBadge`: `AppRadius.full`, padding 8 × 2, the tone's fill at 16% for a positive score and 10% for zero or negative — a negative score is the quality profile doing its job, not a fault, so it reads neutral, never red — the signed figure in `labelSmall` w600 tabular walked against that composite, 4, then *CF* in `labelSmall` at 80%.
- **Title** — 8 below the badges, `bodyMedium` w600 at 1.25 leading, two lines (four when expanded), ellipsised.
- **Meta line** — 8 below, wrapping 12 × 4: `InfoChip`s of a 13pt glyph (`storage_outlined`, `schedule_outlined`, `dns_outlined`), 4, and `labelSmall` tabular in `onSurfaceVariant`, one line each. Torrent peers (`arrow_upward_rounded`, *seeders / leechers*) take `warning` at 0 seeders, `info` under 5, else `success`, walked against the card.
- **Right column** — 8 from the text: Grab, a 48 × 48 box holding a circular `IconButton` — the tone's fill at 14% with a 20pt `download_rounded` in the tone walked against the card (tooltip *Grab release*); 6% fill and disabled while another grab is in flight (tooltip *Another download is starting*); an 18pt, 2pt-stroke spinner in the walked tone while this row's grab runs. Below it, 4, an 18pt `expand_more_rounded` in `onSurfaceVariant` that turns half a turn in 320ms (`AppAnimation.durationSm`) on the standard curve.
- **Expanded** (`AnimatedSize`, 320ms): 12, a 1pt divider in `outlineVariant` at 50%, 12; the full name as `SelectableText` in `bodySmall` `onSurfaceVariant` at 1.35 leading, beside a copy `IconButton` (16pt glyph, tooltip *Copy release name*, toast *Release name copied*). *Custom formats*: 8, a `labelSmall` w600 header in `onSurfaceVariant` with a *Score: +n* pill at its end, 8, the `CustomFormatChip`s wrapping at 8 — padding 8 × 4, `AppRadius.full`, the tone at 12% with a 28% border, name in `labelSmall` `onSurface`, score in `labelSmall` w600 tabular walked against the card (green for a rewarded format, neutral for a penalised one, never red). *Rejection reasons*: 12, the header, then each reason with a 14pt `block_rounded` and `bodySmall`, both neutral — a rejection is the *Arr explaining a decision it already made, not a service that failed to answer. With neither: *No custom format data available*.

**The sheet** (`InteractiveSearchSheet.show` / `.showAsync`, through `AppBottomSheet.showScrollable` at 0.75 initial / 0.5 min / 0.95 max; title *Releases*, subtitle the searched title, `travel_explore_rounded`, the service's accent (the lamp), a close button): a dense filter field (*Filter releases*, `surfaceContainerHigh`, `AppRadius.md`, no border until focused, a 20pt search glyph, a clear button once typed in); a 40pt horizontal chip row — the sort shell (*CF score* / *Size* / *Seeders* / *Age*, 32 tall, `AppRadius.sm`, a trailing arrow that flips the direction), *Approved only*, one chip per protocol when both appear, and an indexer shell (*All indexers*) when more than one answered; then a `labelSmall` summary (*4 releases · 2 approved*, or *2 of 4 releases · 2 approved* with a *Clear* action while filters are on). Rows are sorted by CF score, highest first, by default. Empty: *No releases found* ("No indexer returned a result for this search. Search again later, or check your indexer settings."), or *No releases match filters* with *Clear filters*. Loading (`showAsync`): a 16pt spinner, *Searching for releases…*, the elapsed time from 3 s on (tabular, read on focus and never announced), *Cancel*, then six 104pt skeleton blocks — no percentage, because the server reports none. Grabbing asks first (`showAppConfirmDialog`: *Grab release* or *Grab rejected release*, listing the reasons, confirm *Download release*) and on success closes the sheet under a `primary` *Download started* snackbar.

The consumer provides the release list (or a fetch taking a `CancelToken`), the title, the accent (the lamp) and the grab callback; a callback that decides not to grab throws `ReleaseGrabAbandoned` and the row returns to idle.

Do keep the rail, the Grab and the header count on the one `releaseStatusTone` so the three agree. Don't invent a percentage for a release search.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room; the row also on the desktop.

- `ReleaseListItem`: approved, not approved, rejected; the peers tones; grabbing and another row's grab in flight; expanded (rejected, approved, no custom format data).
- `ScoreBadge`, `CustomFormatChip`, `InfoChip`; `InteractiveSearchSheet` with releases and with none.
- Text at 200%: the three rows and the chips, a rejected row expanded, and the search sheet.
