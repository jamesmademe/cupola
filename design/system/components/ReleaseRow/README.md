# ReleaseRow

One release in the Interactive Search sheet: quality, protocol and score, the release name, size · age · indexer · peers, and a round Grab, colour-railed by what the *Arr would do with it.

Hand-written from `lib/core/widgets/release_list_widgets.dart` (`ReleaseListItem`, `ScoreBadge`, `CustomFormatChip`, `InfoChip`) and `lib/core/widgets/interactive_search_sheet.dart` (the sheet, its toolbar and its loading state). Release names, sizes, indexers and reasons are illustrative.

- Outer padding `space-lg` × `space-xs`. Card: `surface-container-high`, `radius-md`, `level-1`, 1px border `outline-variant` at 50% (rejected: the tone at 28%), clipped. A 3px rail down the left edge in the tone at 90%. Inner padding `space-md` / `space-md` / `space-sm` / `space-md`.
- Tone (`ReleaseStatusTone`, walked with `statusToneColor` against `surface`): approved `success`, not approved `info`, rejected `warning` — never red.
- Badge row, wrapping, `space-xs` apart: quality pill (`primary` at 16%), protocol pill (`on-surface-variant` at 10%, 12px glyph `swap_vert` Torrent / `cloud_download` Usenet), and for rejected a `block` *Rejected* pill in the tone. Every pill's label and glyph is that tone walked by `onTint` against the card's own `surface-container-high` — the fill keeps the raw byte, the ink is measured. Pills: padding `space-sm` × 2, `radius-sm`, `label-small` 600. At the right the `ScoreBadge`: `radius-full`, padding `space-sm` × 2, the tone's fill at 16% (10% for zero or negative — a negative score is the quality profile doing its job, not a fault, so it reads neutral, never red), figure `label-small` 600 tabular walked against that composite on `surface-container-high`, then *CF* at 80%.
- Title: `space-sm` below the badges, `body-medium` 600 at 1.25 leading, two lines (four expanded).
- Meta line: `space-sm` below, wrapping `space-md` × `space-xs`: `InfoChip`s of a 13px outlined glyph (`storage`, `schedule`, `dns`) plus `label-small` tabular in `on-surface-variant`. Torrent peers (`arrow_upward`, *seeders / leechers*) take `warning` at 0 seeders, `info` under 5, else `success`, walked against the card.
- Right column, `space-sm` from the text: Grab, a 48 × 48 tap target holding a 40 × 40 circle — the tone's fill at 14% with a 20px `download` glyph in the tone walked against the card; 6% fill while another grab is in flight (tooltip *Another download is starting*); an 18px spinner in the walked tone while this row's grab runs. Below it, `space-xs`, an 18px `expand_more` that turns 180° in 200ms on the standard curve.
- Expanded (`AnimatedSize`, 200ms): `space-md`, a hairline in `outline-variant` at 50%, `space-md`; the full name in `body-small` `on-surface-variant` (selectable) with a 48-target copy button (toast *Release name copied*); *Custom formats* header with a *Score: +n* pill and `CustomFormatChip`s (padding `space-sm` × `space-xs`, `radius-full`, 12% fill, 28% border, name `label-small` in `on-surface`, score 600 tabular walked against the card — green for a rewarded format, neutral for a penalised one, never red); *Rejection reasons* with a 14px `block` glyph and `body-small` lines, both in `on-surface-variant` — a rejection is the *Arr explaining a decision it already made, not a service that failed to answer.

The sheet (via **AppBottomSheet** `.showScrollable`, 0.75 initial / 0.5 min / 0.95 max, title *Releases*, subtitle = the searched title, `travel_explore`, service accent, close): a 44-tall filter field (*Filter releases*, `surface-container-high`, `radius-md`, 20px search glyph); a 40-tall horizontal chip row — the sort shell (*CF score* / *Size* / *Seeders* / *Age*, 32 tall, `radius-sm`, trailing arrow flips direction), *Approved only*, one chip per protocol when both appear, and an indexer shell when more than one answered; a `label-small` summary (*48 releases · 12 approved*, or *12 of 48 releases · …* with a *Clear* action). Loading: a 16px spinner, *Searching for releases…*, the elapsed time from 3 s on (*0:12*, tabular, read on focus and never announced), *Cancel*, then six 104 skeleton blocks — no percentage, because the server reports none. Empty: *No releases match filters* / *No releases found*. Grabbing asks first (**AppDialog**, *Grab release* / *Grab rejected release*) and closes the sheet under a `primary` *Download started* snackbar.

The consumer provides the release list (or a fetch taking a `CancelToken`), the title, the accent and the grab callback; a callback that decides not to grab throws `ReleaseGrabAbandoned`. Release names, sizes and indexers are illustrative and drawn from the 1922 *Nosferatu*; the indexer names are placeholders (*Example Indexer*, *Example Usenet*, *Example Torrents*) and the release-group tags are `-EXAMPLE`, not any real tracker or group.

Do keep the rail, the Grab and the header count on the one `releaseStatusTone` so the three agree. Don't invent a percentage for a release search.
