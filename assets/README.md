# Assets

What ships inside the app besides code. Nothing here is drawn by hand: each file says where it comes from.

## `brand/`: the Cupola mark

The cupola seen from above, six lit panes around a round window. It is artwork, the one object in the interface that
brings its own colour.

- `cupola_mark.svg` is the vector source of record and `cupola_mark.png` the 1024 px raster the app draws
  (`CupolaMark`, in `lib/core/widgets/service_ring.dart`). `cupola_icon.svg` and `cupola_launcher.svg` are the app icon's
  compositions. All four are generated by the icon pipeline (`scripts/icons/README.md`): change the mark in
  `scripts/icons/masters.py` and run `scripts/generate_icons.sh --write`, never by editing an output.
- **Drawn as it is, on either ground.** The mark keeps its own glass and colour over the dark room and over paper alike;
  it is never redrawn, recoloured, outlined, tinted to an accent or set in a tone. The dark body belongs to the app
  icon, not to the mark.
- **Where it stands:** the centre of the ring (the Welcome, the close of the way in, the Home with nothing set up), the
  launch screen (112 pt on the room's ground), the foot of Settings (40 pt, over the claim) and the licence page
  (56 pt). Nowhere else: a list, a header or any other empty state never wears it.
- The app never draws it below 40 pt; the small app icons, under 64 px, use the pipeline's simplified twin.

## `fonts/`

Bricolage Grotesque, Onest and Geist Mono, each beside its SIL OFL licence (the licence travels with the font, and
`main.dart` registers each one so it appears on the licence page). `CupolaServices.otf` is the service marks font, built
from `tool/service_glyphs/svg/` by `tool/service_glyphs/build_font.py` (see `tool/service_glyphs/README.md`); rebuild
it rather than editing the file. A service mark identifies a service and nothing else, in ink, never in the service's
own colour.
